Saga compensation coordination benchmark · B.Tech project

Who should decide the order
a failed saga unwinds in?

Four ways to coordinate rollback of a distributed transaction, run head to head: a central orchestrator, naive event-driven choreography, and this project's contribution — on-demand leader election paired with a dependency-graph-aware compensation scheduler. 2,510 saga runs, zero randomness, every claim traced back to one CSV.

The one-sentence result

Path D pays a small, bounded latency premium over choreography — 11–15%, flat no matter how wide the rollback gets — and gets full correctness for it. Orchestration pays a premium for the same correctness that keeps growing instead: 1.5× at width 1, 2.9× by width 8. Choreography is fastest and cheapest — and is wrong on every single run, by construction, not by luck.
